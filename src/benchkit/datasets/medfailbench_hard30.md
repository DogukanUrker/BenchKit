# MedFailBench hard-30 dataset notice

This package vendors the 30 prompts from `prompt_set_v2_hard_30.tsv` in
[`goktugozkanmd/medical-ai-failure-atlas`](https://github.com/goktugozkanmd/medical-ai-failure-atlas),
pinned to revision `553584a4eddaf010e1677943e31108f533128686`.

The prompt data is distributed under CC BY 4.0. The BenchKit adapter and its
deterministic scoring rules are distributed under BenchKit's Apache-2.0 license.
The dataset contains synthetic evaluation scenarios, not patient records.

MedFailBench is an automated safety screen. Its scores are not clinical
validation, medical advice, certification, deployment evidence, or a basis for
ranking models for clinical use.
